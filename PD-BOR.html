BigOven - Redesign concept

A redesign focused on simplifying the experience, improving recipe discovery, and creating a clearer, more consistent interface.

Overview

My starting point was reviewing everything I could find about the existing product, App Store reviews, support feedback, competitor apps, and usability issues. That research shaped a revised sitemap and new user flows, which I then built out into wireframes, prototypes, and final UI in Figma.

Role

The redesign focused on modernizing the experience without losing the familiarity and functionality current users relied on — reviewing the product, identifying usability and visual inconsistencies, restructuring key screens, and refining interactions across the board.

Appoach

I focused on removing unnecessary complexity, strengthening the visual hierarchy, and making sure key actions were clear and easy to find rather than buried in the interface. While the redesign was never developed, it reflects how I identify opportunities, build a case for them, and translate complexity into a cohesive, cross-platform experience.
