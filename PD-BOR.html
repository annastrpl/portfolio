BigOven - Product Redesign

A redesign focused on simplifying the experience, improving recipe discovery, and creating a clearer, more consistent interface.
Status: Design completed · Not developed
01 - Understanding the problem

Before redesigning individual screens, I reviewed the product as a whole, using App Store reviews, customer-support feedback, competitor research and existing product knowledge to identify recurring usability problems. I mapped the relationships between the main areas of the experience and created updated user flows and a sitemap, which helped reveal where navigation had become fragmented and where related tasks could be brought closer together.

02 - Simplifying the experience

The redesign focused on simplifying these connections and creating a clearer structure around the most important user goals. Navigation was reorganised to give search, saved recipes and planning tools more distinct roles, while search itself was reconsidered as a broader experience, with improved sorting, filtering and information hierarchy. Information-heavy areas such as the Meal Planner and Grocery List were also restructured to create stronger hierarchy, make key actions easier to identify and reduce visual complexity.

03 - Outcome

The result was a new product direction across responsive web, iOS and Android, bringing greater consistency and clarity to the overall experience. The redesign was presented internally as a broader product proposal and the design work was completed, although it was ultimately not taken into development.
